  🚀 HOW TO USE - FOR PATIENTS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to Patient Dashboard → Lab Reports
2. Find any completed lab report or order
3. Look for the green button with WhatsApp icon
4. Click it
5. WhatsApp opens automatically
6. Message is pre-filled with report information
7. Click SEND in WhatsApp
8. ✅ Done! Message delivered.

  📞 TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK GUIDE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Button not showing?
A: Check if patient has phone on file. Add phone to profile.

Q: WhatsApp not opening?
A: Install WhatsApp (mobile) or login to WhatsApp Web (desktop).

Q: "Invalid phone" error?
A: Ensure phone has at least 10 digits. Example: 9597244055

Q: Message not pre-filled?
A: Clear browser cache, refresh page, try again.

Q: Phone number format wrong?
A: Accepted formats: 10 digits or +919xxxxxxxxxx
