### 1. **Condition Stacking Logic**
For a patient with Diabetes + Hypertension + CKD:
```
Priority 1️⃣: Kidney Safety (irreversible damage)
Priority 2️⃣: Sugar Control (affects kidneys too)
Priority 3️⃣: BP Management (supports both)

Diet rules follow this priority automatically
```

### 2. **Lab-Linked Justification**
```
Patient's Lab: BP = 150/95 (HIGH)
     ↓
System Links: "Reduce sodium to <2300mg daily"
     ↓
Explanation: "Sodium reduction shown to lower BP 5-10mmHg"
     ↓
Result: Patient understands WHY they're on this diet
```
