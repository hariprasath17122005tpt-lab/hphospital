💊 Drug-Nutrient Interactions & Safety Notes

📌 Metformin 1000mg BD

⚠️ INTERACTION:
Vitamin B12 malabsorption (10-30% of long-term users)
→ MANAGEMENT:
Supplement B12: 1000 mcg/week IM or 2000 mcg oral daily
⏰ TIMING:
Take with breakfast & dinner (largest meals); Enhances absorption & reduces GI upset

📌 Amlodipine 5mg OD

⚠️ INTERACTION:
Grapefruit/pomegranate (CYP3A4 inhibition) → increased amlodipine levels
→ MANAGEMENT:
AVOID grapefruit juice entirely; Pomegranate acceptable in small quantities
⏰ TIMING:
Take at evening for steady-state plasma concentration

📌 Atorvastatin 20mg OD

⚠️ INTERACTION:
Cranberry juice (CYP3A4 competition)
→ MANAGEMENT:
Minimize cranberry intake; High-dose fish oil (>3g/day) may potentiate bleeding
⏰ TIMING:
Take at evening (peak HMG-CoA reductase activity); Separate from Ca-rich foods by 2+ hours
