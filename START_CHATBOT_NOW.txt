🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: I see "Error: Could not connect to local AI server"
A: Ollama needs to be running. In PowerShell, type: ollama serve

Q: Model is taking too long to respond
A: First load is slower. Restart Ollama if needed: ollama serve

Q: Model not found error
A: Download it: ollama pull neural-chat (wait 10 minutes)

Q: "Connection refused" error
A: Check port 11434 is not blocked. Run: netstat -ano | findstr ":11434"
