DATASET STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Each Q&A entry contains:
- ID: Unique identifier
- Condition: Medical condition name
- Category: Type (explanation, symptom_recognition, medication, etc.)
- Question: User query variation
- Answer: 5-6 line detailed medical explanation
- Type: Classification (medical_explanation, symptom_management, etc.)

ANSWER FORMAT (5-6 lines):
1. Main explanation (1 line)
2. Causes or related information (1 line)
3. Symptoms or details (1 line)
4. Treatment/Management (1-2 lines)
5. When to seek help/disclaimer (1 line)

EXAMPLE ANSWER FOR FEVER:
"Fever is an increase in body temperature above normal (98.6°F/37°C), 
typically indicating the body is fighting an infection or inflammation.

Causes: Viral infection, Bacterial infection, Fungal infection, 
Autoimmune disease, Heat stroke and more.

Symptoms include: Chills, Sweating, Headache, Muscle aches, Fatigue.

Most cases of fever respond well to home treatment and rest. 
The condition is typically contagious and usually lasts usually 3-7 days.

Seek immediate medical attention if symptoms worsen or emergency signs appear."
